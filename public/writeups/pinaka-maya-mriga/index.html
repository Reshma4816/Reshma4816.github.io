Maya Mriga: The Phantom Trail

Insecure direct object reference (IDOR) chained with cache pollution.

Reshma Al (Coldcoffee) · 15 Feb 2026 · 2 min read

Overview

We get a telemetry client (mayanet-cli), a 24-hour capture of 50,000 events (capture-24h.jsonl), and a live instance. The service keeps two views of a deer’s position:

  • projection, ordered by (ts, retry)
  • authoritative, ordered by seq

/api/validate takes a deer_id, a proof_token and an ordered trail of [x, y] points. It returns the flag if the trail is the real one.

Step 1: Find the real deer

Each event has seq, ts, retry, deer_id, x, y, prev_sig, sig. Grouping by deer gives nine:

  • Eight deer have about 6,200 events each, with random positions and no prev_sig to sig links. They’re noise.
  • deer-d51269dc11cc has 396 events, a chain that starts from an all-zero prev_sig, and smooth movement. This is the target.

Step 2: Rebuild the chain

Start at prev_sig = "0" * 64 and keep picking the event whose prev_sig equals the current sig. That gives a 256-step chain with small, continuous moves. The other 140 events for this deer are decoys with random positions and retry values of 1 to 3. I saved the chain’s points in order as trail.json.

Step 3: Get a proof token

Calling validate without a token gives 403 invalid or missing proof_token -- demonstrate the divergence first, and /api/proof returns 409 until updates are submitted. Two updates whose seq order and ts order disagree make the views diverge:

update --seq 1 --ts 1000 --x 10 --y 10
update --seq 2 --ts 900  --x 20 --y 20

The projection now says (10, 10) and the authoritative view says (20, 20). /api/proof then reports "diverged": true and issues a proof_token.

Step 4: Validate

./mayanet-cli --url https://pinaka-golden-deer.onrender.com --token <session> \
  validate --deer-id deer-d51269dc11cc --proof-token <proof_token> --trail trail.json

This returned the flag.

Takeaway

The decoy deer and retry noise are the illusion. The real deer is the low-volume one with an intact signature chain, and the ordering mismatch between the two views is what unlocks the proof token.
